# Enclosure (CAD)

STEP files for the 3D-printed enclosure that houses the ESP32-C3, SN65HVD230 CAN board and
the 24 V → 5 V buck converter.

Notes:
- Keep the ceramic antenna end of the C3-Zero clear of metal/copper.
- Plan a light pipe/window over the onboard WS2812 (GPIO10) so the status LED is visible.
- Wet/brewing environment: consider conformal coating and/or a breather vent, and route cable
  glands downward so any condensation drains away from the electronics.
